faq

good questions.

os still wondering?

we answer every email ourselves, usually within a day.

hello@outfitspin.app
what happens to my photos?

your closet, outfits and body photos are stored on your phone, not in an outfitspin cloud. when you add a piece or tap wear it, the photo is sent to our ai provider to be tagged or rendered, and the result comes back to your device. we don’t sell your photos or use them for ads. you can wipe everything in one tap under you → clear all data.

do i need photos of myself?

only for try-on. outfit suggestions work without them, and you can skip the body and face photos during setup and add them later.

how many clothes do i need to start?

about 8–10 pieces gives the stylist enough to mix: a few tops, two bottoms, a pair of shoes, one jacket. the more you add, the more combos you’ll get.

will it tell me to buy stuff?

no. every outfit is built only from pieces in your closet. there are no shopping links or sponsored clothes in outfitspin.

how does it know the weather?

set your city once (or allow location) and outfitspin pulls the live forecast for the day, including temperature swings and rain, and dresses you for it.

what’s the difference between spin and remix?

spin (◀ ▶) swaps a single piece, like the shoes, instantly and keeps everything else. remix asks the stylist for a whole new look for the same day.

can i cancel plus?

yes, any time, in your app store or google play subscriptions. cancel during the 7-day trial and you pay nothing.
